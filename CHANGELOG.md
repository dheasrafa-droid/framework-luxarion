## [1.0.0] - 2026-08-15

### Added
- **Core Math Pipeline**: Vector2, Vector3, Vector4, Matrix3, Matrix4, Quaternion, Euler, and Simplex/FBM Noise.
- **Parametric Geometries**: BoxGeometry, SphereGeometry, CylinderGeometry, PlaneGeometry, TorusGeometry, TorusKnotGeometry, IcosahedronGeometry, and Custom BufferGeometry.
- **Material Subsystem**: BasicMaterial, PhongMaterial, HologramMaterial, QuantumMaterial, NormalMaterial, WireframeMaterial, and TextureMaterial.
- **Procedural Texture Generation**: Mathematical generation for Cyber Grids, Simplex Nebula Noise, Hexagonal Honeycombs, Voronoi Crystals, Tangent-Space Normal Bump Maps, and Matrix Canvas Streaming.
- **Lighting Model**: AmbientLight, DirectionalLight, and PointLight with distance attenuation.
- **Camera & Navigation**: PerspectiveCamera, OrthographicCamera, Camera2D, and damped OrbitControls.
- **Dual Pipeline Support**: WebGLRenderer (3D) and Canvas2DRenderer with particle flow field, vector dynamics, and n-body gravitational computing.
- **Theme Orchestrator**: Cyberpunk, Dark Gold, Quantum Neon, Matrix Emerald, and Monolith palettes.
- **18 Interactive Visual Demos**: Galactic Singularity, Procedural Terrains, Quantum Superposition, Orbiting Orreries, Normal Bump Relief, Texture Galleries, and Matrix Glyph Streaming.
- **Full Library Build Output**: UMD, ESM, and TypeScript declaration builds.
- **Comprehensive Unit Testing Suite**: Testing math transforms, geometry generation, scene graphs, color arithmetic, and noise functions.
